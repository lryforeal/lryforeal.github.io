## Biography

Hello! I am **Runyu Lu (陆润宇)**, a Ph.D. Candidate at the **University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS)**, specializing in Computer Applied Technology at the School of Artificial Intelligence (Institute of Automation). My expected graduation date is **June 2026**.

My research is centered around **Game Theory and Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)**, with a focus on developing robust and theoretically grounded algorithms for complex multi-agent systems. I am particularly interested in equilibrium learning theory and its application in real-world adversarial settings.

I hold a B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (2021).

## Academic Highlights

### Research Focus

My primary goal is to develop model-based or offline learning algorithms that offer **last-iterate convergence guarantees** toward approximate Nash Equilibria. This work facilitates stable implementation in deep RL contexts. Key research themes include:

* **Equilibrium Finding Theory** and Last-Iterate Convergence.
* **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)** in Dynamic Games.
* **Equilibrium Policy Generalization** (EPG) for cross-graph robustness.
* Worst-Case Robust Strategies under Partial Observability.
