# Runyu Lu - 陆润宇

Affiliations. Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Applied Technology, **University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS)**.

<hr>

Hello there! I'm Runyu Lu, a Ph.D. Candidate specializing in **Game Theory and Reinforcement Learning** at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS).

My research aims to develop model-based or offline learning algorithms with last-iterate convergence guarantees, which facilitate deep RL implementations. My work focuses on **Equilibrium Finding Theory** and **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning** for Dynamic Games.

Prior to my Ph.D., I earned my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.
